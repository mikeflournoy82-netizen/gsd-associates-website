Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a fractional VP of Sales cost in New York?

A fractional VP of Sales in New York typically costs $8,000–$15,000/month given the market. This compares to $250,000–$350,000/year plus equity for a full-time VP in NYC.

Is a fractional VP of Sales right for my New York SaaS company?

For NYC-based B2B SaaS companies at $1M–$5M ARR dealing with longer enterprise sales cycles and sophisticated buyers, a fractional VP brings the enterprise sales playbook without the full-time New York price tag.

What does a fractional VP of Sales actually do?

They build your outbound pipeline, hire and onboard sales reps, design comp plans, set up your CRM, and run your sales process — typically 10–20 hours/week embedded in your team. Not consulting from a distance. Actually in the trenches.
